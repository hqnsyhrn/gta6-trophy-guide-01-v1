    Roadmap to the Platinum — S1 to S7

    Story first (60–80 h), then cleanup, then the 100% grind. Save at the start and end of every chapter — 15 manual slots, no mission replay, no New Game+.

    100% completion — what actually counts

    100% is about 60% of the game's true total content. The counted parts: 200 of 277 vehicles scanned with the Waink app · 100 of 255 animals spotted with binoculars · 300 counted stunts (130 stunt jumps, 202 under-the-bridge, 40 knife flights, 25 base jumps — 397 exist) · 70 of 80 challenges · 100 optional missions. Trust the in-game tracker over any guide.

    Missables & Watch

    ⚠ THE ONE POSSIBLY MISSABLE — Positive Mental Attitude (trophy 28)

    Chapter 1, after the Down to Earth mission: in the morning, find Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept to meditate. It adds +1 to the meditation counter but is not a marked spot.

    SOURCES DISAGREE — both versions shown: (A) 18 meditations at 10 named places are needed. (B) 100% completion only needs 15, so the trophy may need fewer than 18. Do all 18 to be safe. Named spots: Capri sandbar, Buckskin Swamp, Camp Moonflower, Manifest Canyon, Moreau wheel, Kalaga mine tower, Tallaloko falls, Waning Sands rocks, Mount Kalaga summit.

    Save discipline — read this before you start

    No mission replay. No New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving. 15 manual save slots — keep one save at the start and one at the end of every chapter, plus one right before the final mission and one before every major story choice from Chapter 4 onward.
